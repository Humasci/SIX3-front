# SIX³ positioning and offers

The single source for how SIX³ describes itself. Website copy, blog posts, LinkedIn and decks should follow it.

## Who we're for

UK small and mid-sized businesses that grow through leads and online sales: growing e-commerce brands, law firms, clinics and healthcare practices, estate agents, professional services firms and local businesses. They usually already spend on marketing but lose value between the click and the customer: slow replies, missed calls, abandoned baskets, thin content, ads nobody optimises.

## The proposition

**Tagline: Growth, cubed.**

Supporting line: More leads in, every lead answered.

SIX³ is a Brighton AI marketing and automation agency. We combine three things most businesses buy from three different suppliers, and make them work as one system:

1. **Get found:** search and paid media that bring in the right customers.
2. **Get chosen:** AI-produced video, voice and ad content that makes people pick you.
3. **Get it done:** AI agents and automations that answer, qualify and book every lead, day and night.

Each one multiplies the others. Better ads bring more leads, better content converts more of them, and automation makes sure none are lost. That's the ³: growth that compounds instead of adding up.

### One-liner
We help service businesses get found, get chosen and get more done, with AI marketing and automation that work as one system.

### Why us
- **One team, one system.** Ads, content and automation are planned together, measured together and reported together.
- **Built by operators.** We plan, build and run everything ourselves, so nothing gets lost between suppliers.
- **Practical AI.** We use AI where it saves time or wins customers, not as a buzzword. Every build has a clear job and a number attached.
- **Brighton-based, since 2018.** Senior people on every account.

### Tone of voice
Plain, confident, specific. Short sentences. Talk about the customer's outcome before the technology. No hype words ("revolutionise", "cutting-edge", "unprecedented"). Never invent statistics: only use numbers we can back up, or describe what a system does (for example "answers every enquiry in under a minute").
